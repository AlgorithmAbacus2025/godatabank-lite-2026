# Source Plan

## Research question

What does the 2024 ONS cohort-fertility release show about completed family
size, timing of births, and projected family size for different birth cohorts
in England and Wales?

## Discovery lead — not evidence

GovDelivery ONS newsletter:

https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/UKONS/bulletins/41e3578

The newsletter was used only to locate the ONS release.

## Primary evidence

1. ONS statistical bulletin, **Fertility for those born in different years,
   England and Wales: 2024**, released 10 June 2026.
2. ONS dataset, **Fertility for those born in different years, England and
   Wales**, latest XLSX released 10 June 2026.
3. ONS quality and methods guide for the release series.

## Decision

Use the bulletin for the published interpretation and headline percentages.
Use the downloaded workbook to verify completed and achieved family-size
figures and to distinguish observed data from projections.
